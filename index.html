Synevra UltraLift™ Ingredients

Synevra UltraLift™ is marketed as a skincare system combining a topical peptide serum with an oral beauty supplement. The serum focuses on skin hydration and the appearance of fine lines, while the companion supplement contains nutrients intended to support healthy-looking skin.

1

Dipeptide Complex (SYN-AKE®)

SYN-AKE® is a synthetic peptide used in cosmetic skincare formulations. It is designed to help soften the appearance of expression lines and promote smoother-looking skin. Its effects depend on the formulation and concentration, and it should not be considered equivalent to injectable treatments.
2

Sodium Hyaluronate

Sodium hyaluronate is a form of hyaluronic acid that attracts and retains moisture. It helps hydrate the skin, improve its supple appearance, and temporarily make fine lines look less noticeable by increasing surface hydration.
3

Vitamin C (Ascorbic Acid)

Vitamin C is an antioxidant commonly used in skincare products. It helps protect against oxidative stress and can support a brighter, more even-looking complexion. Its effectiveness depends on the concentration, formulation stability, and how the product is used.
4

Vitamin E (Tocopheryl Acetate)

Tocopheryl acetate is a form of vitamin E frequently included in cosmetic formulations. It provides antioxidant benefits and helps condition the skin, supporting a softer feel and a healthy-looking skin barrier.
5

Glycerin

Glycerin is a humectant that attracts water and helps maintain skin hydration. It can reduce the feeling of dryness, improve softness, and leave the skin feeling smoother and more comfortable.
6

Biotin

Biotin, also known as vitamin B7, is an essential nutrient involved in normal metabolic processes. It is included in the oral beauty supplement as part of its nutritional support approach. Additional biotin may not improve skin appearance in people who already get enough from their diet.
7

Niacinamide (Vitamin B3)

Niacinamide is a form of vitamin B3 widely used in skincare and nutritional products. When applied topically, it can support the skin barrier and improve the appearance of uneven skin tone. In an oral supplement, its role is nutritional and depends on the amount provided.
8

Vitamin B6

Vitamin B6 contributes to normal protein metabolism and various cellular processes. It is included in the companion beauty supplement to provide nutritional support. Excessive long-term intake of vitamin B6 can cause nerve damage, so the dosage matters.
9

Zinc

Zinc is an essential mineral that contributes to normal immune function, protein synthesis, and wound healing. It also plays a role in maintaining healthy skin. Supplementation is most beneficial when it addresses an actual nutritional shortfall.
10

Horsetail Extract

Horsetail extract is a botanical ingredient traditionally associated with silica content. It is included in some beauty supplements marketed for skin, hair, and nail wellness. However, strong clinical evidence that oral horsetail extract improves skin firmness or reduces wrinkles remains limited.
